# Acceptable Use Policy

**AgentBreaker** is an offensive AI security research framework designed to assist security professionals, machine learning engineers, and researchers in identifying, assessing, and mitigating vulnerabilities in LLMs and AI agent workflows.

## Permitted Uses

You may use AgentBreaker exclusively for:
1. **Authorized Red Teaming & Testing:** Conducting security assessments against systems, models, and endpoints for which you have explicit, written authorization.
2. **Defensive Research & Alignment:** Benchmarking AI guardrails, evaluating prompt injection mitigations, and testing alignment mechanisms on internal or research models.
3. **Academic & Educational Research:** Controlled study of AI safety, adversarial attacks, and robust system architectures in compliance with applicable laws and institution guidelines.

## Prohibited Uses

You are strictly prohibited from using AgentBreaker to:
1. Attack, probe, disrupt, or evaluate any third-party model, API, agent, or service without prior explicit authorization from the system owner.
2. Generate, propagate, or facilitate illegal, malicious, or abusive activities, malware distribution, data exfiltration, or harassment.
3. Attempt to bypass or defeat safety guardrails on public consumer AI products without vendor bug bounty authorization.
4. Resell or redistribute attack payloads for unauthorized malicious targeting.

By using AgentBreaker, you agree to comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws, regulations, and third-party terms of service.
